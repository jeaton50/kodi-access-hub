Just arrived somewhere new?

Work through these in order. Most new-location problems are one of the first three.

  1. Check Tailscale on the device you are watching from. Reaching the Tailscale website proves you have internet, not that this device is on the tailnet. The box can be perfectly healthy while your phone or laptop is simply not connected.
  2. Do not mix the hotspot and the private address. A box’s own Wi-Fi has no internet, so Tailscale cannot connect over it. On the hotspot, use that box’s hotspot link rather than its private address.
  3. Wake the home library. Playing from the iMac needs it awake and connected. A sleeping Mac drops off the tailnet, so nothing from the library will play even though the Kodi box is fine.
  4. Confirm the box reached the new Wi-Fi. Hotel, rental and campground networks often show a sign-in page first, which the box cannot accept on its own. It then looks connected but has no internet.
  5. Rescan the HD tuner if it travelled with you. The tuner is a separate device, so it has to join the new network too. A new location receives different broadcast channels: rescan on the tuner itself, then refresh the channel list in Kodi, or the guide keeps showing the old area’s channels. A tuner left at home still carries the home area’s channels and reaches you over Tailscale.
A dashboard isn’t opening?

Confirm Tailscale is connected and the Kodi box is powered on. Try the same link in your regular browser if an in-app browser cannot reach it. When using a box’s own Wi-Fi hotspot, use the hotspot link on its card instead.
